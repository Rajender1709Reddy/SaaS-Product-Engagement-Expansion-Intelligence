# SaaS Product Engagement & Expansion Intelligence

## Project Overview

SaaS Product Engagement & Expansion Intelligence is a comprehensive data analytics project designed to analyze customer subscriptions, product engagement, and business performance within a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) environment. The project leverages Excel, SQL, Python (Pandas), and Power BI to clean, transform, analyze, and visualize customer and operational data, enabling data-driven business decisions.

---

# Project Objectives

- Analyze subscription performance and customer engagement.
- Monitor key business KPIs including revenue, subscriptions, and customer growth.
- Identify customer adoption patterns across regions and plan categories.
- Evaluate pricing strategy using discount and payment analysis.
- Build reusable SQL queries and business KPIs.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard for business intelligence.
